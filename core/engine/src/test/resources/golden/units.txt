# Unit arithmetic and conversion, docs/GRAMMAR.md §3.8.
# Never edit a line here to make a test pass (docs/GRAMMAR.md §5).

locale=en-US

# Addition keeps the left operand's unit
5 km + 300 m                | 5.3 km
300 m + 5 km                | 5300 m
5 km - 300 m                | 4.7 km

# Multiplication and division compose dimensions symbolically
2 h * 60 km/h               | 120 km
100 m / (10 s)              | 10 m/s
2 km * 3                    | 6 km

# A dimensionless value combines freely
5 km + 2                    | 7 km

# Incompatible dimensions are rejected
5 km + 3 kg                 | !UnitMismatch@0..10

# Absolute temperatures are affine
20°C + 5°C                  | !UnitMismatch@0..9
20°C + 5Δ°C                 | 25 °C
30°C - 20°C                 | 10 Δ°C

# Conversion
100°C to °F                 | 212 °F
0°C to K                    | 273.15 K
5 km to m                   | 5000 m
2 h to min                  | 120 min

# `in` is the inch unit only in unit position, docs/GRAMMAR.md §3.3
5 in 3                      | !Syntax@5..5

# `in` after a conversion keyword is the inch: a target cannot be a conversion (§3.3)
5 cm to in                  | 1.968503937007874015748031496062992 in
1 m^2 to in^2               | 1550.0031000062000124000248000496 in^2
