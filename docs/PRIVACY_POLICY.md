# Privacy Policy for Lineo

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-24

Lineo ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in the Lineo Android application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Lineo is a local-first application.** 

- **Calculation Data:** All expressions, variables, and notepad documents you create are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, upload, or have access to your calculation data.
- **Personal Information:** We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information (such as name, email, or phone number) to use the app.

## 2. Permissions

Lineo requests the following permissions for specific features:

- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Used to fetch current currency exchange rates and to serve advertisements (in the free tier). Currency requests do not carry any user identifiers.

## 3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

To improve the app's stability and performance, we may collect anonymous technical data:

- **Crash Logs:** If the app crashes, we collect a report containing the error type and the position in the expression where the error occurred. **We never log the content of your expressions.**
- **Usage Statistics:** We may collect anonymous events about which features are used (e.g., "opened scientific module") to help us prioritise development.

## 4. Data Safety

- **No Data Sharing:** We do not share any user data with third parties, except for anonymous technical data used by the advertisement SDK (if enabled).
- **Data Security:** All network communication (e.g., fetching exchange rates) is performed over secure HTTPS connections.
- **Data Deletion:** Since your data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's cache/data or uninstalling the application.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
